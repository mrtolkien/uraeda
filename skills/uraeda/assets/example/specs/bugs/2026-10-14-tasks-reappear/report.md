# Completed tasks reappear after reload

## What happens

I complete a task, it gets crossed out. When I reload the page, it is not completed anymore.

## What should happen

The task stays completed after a reload.

## How to reproduce

1. Create a task
2. Complete it right away, in less than a second
3. Reload the page

## Environment

Firefox 131, on a laptop. It does not happen every time.
