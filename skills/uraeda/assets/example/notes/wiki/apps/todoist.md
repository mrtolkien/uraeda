# Todoist

A to-do app whose repeats are typed in the due date field, in plain words.

## Facts

- A rule is typed in the due date field: "every 3 months", "every monday"
  ([Todoist help](https://todoist.com/help/articles/introduction-to-recurring-dates-YUYVJJAV)).
- A recurring task is a single task: completing it moves its due date to the next occurrence. The
  list never grows, and past completions leave it (same source).
- "every" counts the next date from the previous due date, "every!" from the day the task was
  completed. A task overdue past its next date moves to the next date in the future (same source).
- One occurrence is moved by rescheduling the task, without changing the rule
  ([user guide, 2016](https://tomtalks.blog/2016/09/changing-due-date-todoist-reoccurring-task-without-breaking-reoccurrence/)):
  an old source, not checked against today's menus.
