# Review the overall flow

## What

Once I'm happy with individual pages and their internal flow, review the overall flow of all user
stories, end to end: the links between pages, the transitions from one state to the next (Inbox >
Priorities > Draft > Change > Feature), and the dead ends.

## Why

Each page can be good on its own and the whole still feel disjointed.

## Notes

- Comes after the page redesigns and the settings page, and before the
  [look and feel pass](look-and-feel.md).
