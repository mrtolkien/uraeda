# Lists and starts in the terminal look

## What

The inbox, the priorities, a feature's page, the session page, and the New page, redrawn in the look
and shell of [layout and look](../done/2026-10-20-layout-look.md).

- **Inbox**: note readability and hierarchy first; notes shown whole, reorderable, in categories
  that can be created and deleted, with a note's actions beside it.
- **Priorities**: Ongoing, Now, Next, and Later as columns on wide and semi-wide screens, kanban
  style, and a vertical list on the phone; menu actions to move a line, beside dragging.
- **A feature's page**: the progress timeline, its state, its sessions, metrics, and better access
  to its files. The Storybook link goes.
- **Session**: the standard chat look, messages first, sources and changes second.
- **New**: three clearly different choices, so a new user picks the right one the first time.
