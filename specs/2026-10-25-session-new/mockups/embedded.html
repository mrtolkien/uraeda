Spec to review, about 15 minutes.

Decisions · 4

New: Repeating tasks. A task repeats by a rule, and completing it moves it to its next due date.

Changed: Task storage. Data MUST be stored in IndexedDB, and an upgrade keeps every earlier version.

Why not keep localStorage for small lists?
04:02 PM
40 s · read 2 files · thought once

localStorage holds about 5 MB per origin and blocks the page on every write. A user with a few thousand tasks and their history passes that limit, and the upgrade in this spec writes every task at once.
