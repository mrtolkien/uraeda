# Dedicated session view

## What

There should be a _dedicated_ session view, not just a sidebar, even on desktop.

Also even when we’re not the ones running the agent the current task of the plan should be viewable
in the sidebar (Task 7/11 or stg like this): the checkmarks in the plan should cover it

## Why

Following what an agent does is one of the main things the UI is for, and the sidebar is too small
for it.

It’s hard to really follow what is happening at the moment.
