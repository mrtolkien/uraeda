While Vim is on, the mode opens the status line: its full name, its icon, its colour. Outside a field it is NAVIGATION, the page's keys. With Vim off, nothing is drawn there.

The switch applies at once. The commands save on Save or Ctrl+Enter, and a line the Vim engine refuses shows under the field, with the engine's own message.

Keyboard

Single-key shortcuts

The leader key, g sequences, and the letter keys of every page.

Vim keys

Vim's keys in every text field, with the mode in the status line. Escape in normal mode leaves the field; Shift+Escape leaves from any mode. Off on touch screens.
