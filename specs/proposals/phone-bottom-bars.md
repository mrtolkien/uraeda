# Phone bottom bars on more pages

## What

On the phone, the round, the spec's pages, and the session page end with a bottom bar: icon tiles on
frosted glass, one for each companion the wide screen's side column has, each opening a sheet, then
the one action that moves the human forward (Next, Verdict) as an accent icon. Other pages have
none. Which pages get one, and what goes in it, needs mockups to choose.

Candidates:

- The feature page: its Produced and Links column is drawn inline on the phone, and could become
  tiles, beside its Session and its next step.
- Pages with one main action, which could end a bar with it: the draft editor's Start, the inbox's
  and the priorities' Add, the commit page's Commit.

## Why

The phone's bottom bar is where the thumb is, and it puts a page's companions and its next action in
the same place on every page. Pages without one hide those in their body or at their top.

## Notes

- How the bar looks and behaves: [the phone](../ui/phone.md) and [surfaces](../ui/surfaces.md).
- No filled accent block on glass: the bar's main action is an accent icon.
