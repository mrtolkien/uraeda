# Pages look like 1.4 again

The rework for v3 was nice and made the bin light and easy

But man the pages look empty and boring and hard to read

We should go back to almost the same look for reading specs and clarifications as uraeda 1.4

And also, there should be a refresh button on the home page

## Asked in the session

How close to 1.4 should the round and spec pages get? 1.4 had: the dark purple/teal/orange palette,
JetBrains Mono titles and labels, square framed boxes with a label cut into the border (Example, Why
it matters, A ★ Recommended), and at a desk an outline column with the progress bar.

**Answer:** "All of it, system fonts": the palette, the framed boxes, and the desk's outline column,
with titles in the system's monospace font, so nothing is added to the binary. No app sidebar, no
status bar.
