# Clarifications during implementation can resume implementation

It's a small workflow change: during implementation, a clarification can EITHER go back to specs
approval OR continue the implementation.

The standard flow will be continuing implementation: all clarifications asked during implementation
were small enough to continue it after my review.

This means there should be a new button at the end of clarifications if they run during an
implementation: "Answer and continue implementing", and its look should signify it's the primary
button.
