Q1: What becomes of the three notes on reading specs?

"Specs are still way too long", "Allow no selection in responses", and "Only true uraeda flows should show up in the UI" are all about reading and answering workflow files. The look and feel draft already gathers six such notes.

A (recommended). Add the three to the draft "Look and feel pass". [pro] one place for UI polish. [con] the draft grows to nine items.
B. Make a new draft, "Reading workflow files", in Next, from the three notes. [pro] a focused feature. [con] overlaps the look and feel pass.
C. Keep them in the inbox. [pro] nothing changes. [con] they stay unsorted.
