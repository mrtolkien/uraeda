Story embeds

The stories inside the frames are drawn by hand here: in the product, each frame is the worktree's own Storybook, running the story live. The example spec is a future feature of Uraeda, "Sidebar resize", whose stories are in packages/web/src/SidebarHandle.stories.tsx.

1. A story embedded in a spec, 1440 × 900, at both widths

The spec's line [Dragging the handle](/packages/web/src/SidebarHandle.stories.tsx#Dragging), alone in its paragraph under US1, shows as the live story, twice: the phone width, 402 px and unscaled, next to the desktop width, 1440 px scaled to the space left. Each frame runs on its own. "Phone" and "Desktop" show one alone, the desktop scaled to the whole column. Frames come from Uraeda's own address, /r/uraeda/preview/2026-10-20-sidebar-resize/. "Pick an element" appears once the frames say they have the pick script.

2. Pick mode, then the comment, 1440 × 900

"Pick an element" is pressed: the story stops reacting to clicks, the element under the pointer is outlined, and a click picks it. Escape, inside or outside the frame, stops pick mode. The comment form opens under the embed, naming the story and the picked element. Ctrl+Enter or "Add comment" saves it to the spec's review, on the embed's line.
