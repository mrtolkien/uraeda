# Record: In vim mode, visual mode seems to be working properly... except selected text…

## What and why

With Vim mode on, the human found that visual mode selected text without highlighting it. They also
found the editor's font "too aggressive", asking to "go back to a standard monospace one", the
status line "way too tall", and its line and column count, with Vim's "All", distracting. While the
change was built, they asked for their own messages in a session to default to "the normal monaspace
font and not the weird handwriting one".

## Behaviour

- **Visual mode highlights its selection**, with Vim on: VISUAL and V-LINE draw it in the accent,
  Catppuccin's mauve as the mode's colour, at 25% over the field, 12% in a field without the focus.
  V-BLOCK selects and draws a block, one range a line, and its operators, like `d`, act on each
  line. With Vim off, a field keeps the browser's selection, in grey, and a single range.
- **The status line has no cursor's place**: no "line:column" and no "All", "Top", "Bot", or
  percentage. It ends with the counts of what needs the human and what runs, in a field as outside.
- **The status line is 24 px tall** on semi-wide and wide screens, one row of its text, down from
  38.4 px. What sits on it, like the leader menu and the commit toast, follows. The phone's bar
  keeps the tabs' height.
- **Code, keys, and every text field default to JetBrains Mono**, the "Code and keys" role, whose
  help now names the text typed. Monaspace Krypton stays a choice.
- **The human's sent messages default to Monaspace Neon**, the interface's font, in their own role.
  Radon stays a choice. A browser where fonts were picked keeps its picks; `Space u f d` restores
  the defaults.

## Design

The Vim engine, `@replit/codemirror-vim`, hides the browser's selection and expects CodeMirror's
`drawSelection()` to draw one, as `basicSetup` does, and sets a block as several ranges, which
CodeMirror reduces to its main one unless `EditorState.allowMultipleSelections` is on. The shared
editor had neither. The Vim layer, [`vim/vim.ts`](/packages/web/src/fields/editor/vim/vim.ts), adds
both, so they come and go with Vim's compartment; the colour is in
[`theme.ts`](/packages/web/src/fields/editor/theme.ts), mixed from `--color-accent` so it follows
both schemes.

The cursor's place left the status line ([`StatusLine.tsx`](/packages/web/src/shell/StatusLine.tsx))
and with it every part that fed it: the `Place` section, lualine's section `z` and the leftward
separator ([`Sections.tsx`](/packages/web/src/shell/status/Sections.tsx)), the place in the mode
store ([`modeStore.ts`](/packages/web/src/fields/editor/modeStore.ts)), and the editor's measure of
the cursor on each key and scroll ([`Editor.tsx`](/packages/web/src/fields/editor/Editor.tsx)). The
line's height is one variable, `--size-status-line`, set in
[`Shell.tsx`](/packages/web/src/Shell.tsx). The font defaults are `DEFAULT_FONTS` in
[`fonts.ts`](/packages/web/src/theme/fonts.ts): the editor keeps the code role's font rather than
one of its own, as the [UI look](../decisions/ui-look.md) decision sets text in the font picked for
its role. The [reading](../ui/reading.md), [surfaces](../ui/surfaces.md), and [wide](../ui/wide.md)
guides and the [web UI's docs](/docs/app.md) say the same.

## Replaced behaviour

- **The status line's cursor's place**, "Top 3:9" at its right end, from
  [Vim in text edit mode](2026-11-03-vim-text.md): guarded by `status-line.test.tsx`'s mode test,
  whose place assertions were removed.
- **Krypton for code and fields, Radon for the human's messages**, the fonts' defaults.

## Gotchas

- **The Vim layer adds `drawSelection()` and `allowMultipleSelections`**: `vim()` alone hides the
  native selection and draws none, and a V-BLOCK selection collapses to one range without the facet
  ([`vim/vim.ts`](/packages/web/src/fields/editor/vim/vim.ts)). Both stay out of the editor without
  Vim, where the browser's selection and a single range are kept.

## Changes after approval

- The review found visual block mode still selecting one line's range: the change made it select and
  draw the block.
- The review found that three new tests guarded only what the sandbox shows, the line's end and the
  two font defaults, against the Testing decision: they were removed, and the selection's two
  reproduction tests stay.
- A selection in a field without the focus is fainter, 12% rather than 25%.
