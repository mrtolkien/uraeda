# Record: Why is the right border so much bigger than the left

> [!NOTE]
>
> The record of a merged change, not instructions to follow. Started on 2026-11-03, finished on
> 2026-11-03, in [its pull request](https://github.com/mrtolkien/uraeda/pull/88). Its full files are
> in git: `git log -- specs/changes/2026-11-03-why-right/`.

## What and why

The human asked why the right border is so much bigger than the left, and to fix it. Their
screenshot showed a round of questions on an iPhone: each line of prose started about 8 px after the
pane's left border, but stopped 40 to 180 px before its right one, so the text looked pushed left in
its pane.

## Behaviour

- **Prose wraps the plain way on every browser**: each line takes as many words as fit, so its text
  ends near the pane's right border on Safari, as it already did on Chrome and Firefox. In WebKit at
  402 px wide, a round's Goal paragraph now stops 10 to 13 px short of the right border, against 44
  to 72 px before.
- **The lint refuses `pretty` and `balance`** for `textWrap` and `textWrapStyle` in a StyleX style:
  a later view cannot bring the ragged edge back without the lint failing.

## Design

The root cause was `text-wrap: pretty` on prose's two styles, `proseStyles.reading` and
`proseStyles.doc`, in [`Prose.tsx`](/packages/web/src/Prose.tsx), which every page's prose goes
through. Chrome reads `pretty` as "avoid a short last line" and touches only a paragraph's last
lines. Safari evens out every line of the paragraph, shortening each one to match the others. The
pane itself is symmetric, 5 to 6 px from each edge of the screen, with no overflow or scrollbar
gutter: only the wrapping was uneven.

- [`Prose.tsx`](/packages/web/src/Prose.tsx) no longer sets `textWrap`.
- `wrapLimit` in [`vite.config.ts`](/vite.config.ts) adds a `stylex/valid-styles` limit on
  `textWrap` and `textWrapStyle`: `auto`, `wrap`, `nowrap`, and `stable` only. The fixture
  [`__lint__/pretty-wrap.tsx`](/packages/web/src/__lint__/pretty-wrap.tsx) and
  [`lint.test.ts`](/packages/web/src/__tests__/lint.test.ts) check that the limit refuses `pretty`.
- The [reading guide](/specs/ui/reading.md#line-length) says that text wraps the plain way, and why.

Unchanged: the reading measure, the fonts, the line height, and every pane's padding and border.

## Gotchas

- **The lint sees StyleX styles only**: Astryx's `Text` and `Heading` take a `textWrap` prop that
  can set `pretty` or `balance` as well, and no rule refuses it. No view passes it today
  ([`vite.config.ts`](/vite.config.ts)).
- **The limit's `reason` is never printed**: StyleX's `valid-styles` rule shows its own message,
  "textWrap value must be one of: …", so the explanation lives in the config's comment and the
  reading guide ([`vite.config.ts`](/vite.config.ts)).
