## What and why

The spec is the workflow's main review, and it was still drawn in Astryx cards of the old look,
inside the new shell of [layout and look](2026-10-20-layout-look.md). The human asked for the spec,
the plan, and the data model in the terminal look: the full spec in its format, each part drawn its
own way, decision changes first and most visible since they shape every later feature, live mockups
clearly framed as embeds, and navigation second, with comments and the session beside the page, as
on the [questions page](2026-10-24-questions-page.md). For the plan, they asked for the least work:
"The plan is either read before tasks start or never".

## Behaviour

- **The spec in titled panes (US1).** Every part of a spec is a pane of the UI look, with its icon,
  in one column at every width.
  - The block is a pane titled by its first line, "Spec to review, revision 2, about 10 minutes."
    (or "Approved on …" once approved), its "You approve" item large and first, then its other items
    and the text before the first section. Under a separator at its foot: the time and risk tier as
    pills, the Constitution check's principles as a strip of pills, and the risk tier's reason. The
    strip takes a comment on its table's row of checks, as any block does.
  - Then "Your review, answered", then "Decisions · 1 new, 2 changed, 1 removed", counting every
    decision pane drawn, the unlisted changes included; then "User stories", "Data", "How it is
    built", and History, folded, its records not mounted while folded. A pane's count is in its
    title ("Edge cases · 5"); its region keeps the part's name.
  - The niche edge cases fold under "N niche cases" inside their pane.
  - "Data" draws the core's sections of `data-model.md` after the stories: its intro in no pane,
    then a pane per entity, a row's "Change" cell a green, yellow, or red badge, that column first
    on the phone. The data model opened alone draws the same panes.
  - An approved spec draws the same panes, without the Review tab or the verdict.
  - On the phone, the block's, decisions', and stories' panes keep only their titled top border, so
    their text and live windows start at the page's 14.4 px inset. No part's head sticks, and no
    band separates the groups. A pane inside a pane keeps its borders.
- **Decision changes as a ledger (US2).** One card draws a decision's change on every page that
  shows one: the spec, the decisions page's branch change, a decision's own page, candidates, the
  decisions check, and a done feature's record.
  - The pane is green when new, mauve when changed, red when removed, titled by the decision with
    its kind's pill; "Not listed in the spec" for a change the spec does not list.
  - Rules not kept come first, each with a sign named "Added" (`+`), "Changed" (`~`), or "Removed"
    (`−`). A changed rule's added words are underlined (wavy) in `<ins>`, its removed words struck
    in `<del>`. A new decision's rules read plain with `+`, a removed one's struck with `−`.
  - Unchanged rules fold under "N rules unchanged", the other sections under "Why, alternatives,
    consequences · N changed"; a pending comment opens its fold. "The whole decision, on its page"
    links at the pane's foot.
  - A spec with no decision listed or changed shows the relied-on table only.
- **Live mockups in a live window (US3).** A story, or an HTML mockup linked alone in its paragraph
  of any workflow file or inbox draft, draws in a pane with a double sapphire border on a hatched
  stage.
  - Its title is "live · <name>", then "· <width> px · <scale>%" once measured, with a phone's or a
    monitor's icon by its width. Full screen shows it at 100%, as an overlay where the browser has
    none.
  - An HTML mockup loads with its link's query and anchor, at the width its `?w=` names, 1440 px
    without one, in the mockup page's sandbox. One that fails reads "This mockup could not be
    loaded." with a link to its page.
  - A story keeps its Phone and Desktop switch at every width, as named icons; each width shows at
    its real size, scaled to fit (402 px at about 90% on the phone, 1440 px at about 25%). A picked
    element's comment names the width it was picked at.
  - On a wide screen a window grows past its column only where that side has at least the 16 px gap
    of room: inside a spec's or a plan's pane, it stays off the pane's border.
- **Contents, Review, and Session beside the spec and the plan (US4).** The questions page's
  document column, labelled "The spec's column" or "The plan's column", at its width rule (480 px at
  1440 px), with Contents open first, Review while a review waits, and Session.
  - Contents and the round's Outline are one list of rows: wrapping titles, nested rows, ✓ or ○, a
    kind's mark, pending comments, Δ for a part changed since your review, the part at the top
    marked. A row's jump focuses its pane's title (`<id>-title`).
  - Review lists the pending comments by their places, a tap bringing each block to the top, then
    "General comment" and "Request changes" and "Approve". Its buttons commit what the page's end
    set: the summary, the risk tier, and plan review live in the review, not in a mounted form.
  - "Ask the agent" is offered on every selection, a decision's parts included. Wide, it opens
    Session with "About specs/decisions/task-storage.md, lines 15–16:" then the part's words,
    without markdown; a removed part reads "About the removed rule of …", from the default branch's
    file. Asking with the column folded unfolds it. On the phone, it opens the session's page with
    the quote, and Back returns to the block.
  - No agent, or a feature without a branch: no Session tab and no "Ask the agent" on the spec and
    the plan. The questions page keeps its blocked Session tab there.
  - On the phone, no column: the status line holds "Contents", the comment count, and "Verdict". The
    open sheet, there as on the round, is the address's `?tab=`: a reload keeps it, Back closes it,
    and crossing to a wide screen opens that tab. The status line's counts ("N need you", "N
    running") are left out where they have no room beside the page's controls.
  - A comment typed stays, marked unsaved, through a tab switch or a width change.
- **What changed since your review (US5).** While a revised spec waits for review, a pane "Since
  your review · N parts changed" sits right under the block, before "Your review, answered", and
  Contents lists it after "What you approve".
  - It lists each changed place in the page's order, named by its Contents entry or its place in a
    story ("US2's journey", "US2, key scenario 1"), a tap jumping to it; removed parts fold, struck,
    under "N parts removed". Its text: "Revision N is the one you reviewed. Each part changed since
    is marked Δ in the page and in Contents."
  - Each changed block, wherever its part draws it, reads "Δ Changed since your review" with a bar
    down its side, its words drawn as runs, added underlined, removed struck. A part whose words
    hold a link, an image, or code keeps its drawing, marked. A changed Constitution check marks its
    strip, a Δ after each changed principle.
  - No pane on a first revision, after an approval, or on an approved spec. "No part changed since
    your review." when only the frontmatter changed; "The version you reviewed is not in git
    anymore." when the review's commit is gone. The opening callout and `## History` are not parts.
- **The plan page (US6).** A plan reads in its file's order, waiting for review or not: its status
  and title, then its "Goal", "Architecture", and "Spec" lead paragraphs as panes, each section a
  pane, each task a pane titled "Task N · <title>" with its `done/total` badge. The column beside it
  holds Contents, Review while it waits, and Session. A plan with no tasks draws its sections.

## Design

The core owns every reading: the data model, the plan's lead, the HTML embeds, and the change since
the review, built on the decision change's own cutting and matching. The shared pane owns every
titled border, its tones included. One card owns how a decision's change reads; the live window owns
how a mockup reads; the questions page's column owns the tabs and the width.

## Questions

### Round 01

#### Q1: How should a decision's change read?

**Answer:** A, a ledger: each decision a strong pane, its rules lines with a sign, changed words
marked inside, unchanged rules and the Why folded with their count.

#### Q2: How should a live mockup show that it is an embed?

**Answer:** A, a live window: a double border in another colour, titled "live" with the mockup's
name, size, and scale, on a hatched background.

#### Q3: What should the right column hold beside the session?

**Answer:** A, three tabs, Contents, Review, and Session, Review listing the comments and holding
the verdict's two buttons; the full verdict stays at the page's end.

#### Q4: What should a revision show of the changes since your last review?

**Answer:** A, a "Since your review" pane listing the changed parts, each marked Δ in the page and
in Contents, its words diffed as a decision's change is.

#### Q5: What should the plan page put first?

**Answer:** B, the file's order, each section a pane: "The plan is either read before tasks start or
never", so "the least work".

## Changes after approval

- The Constitution check's principles and the risk tier's reason sit after a separator at the block
  pane's foot, as the approval's general comment asked; the strip takes a comment and is marked when
  it changed since your review.
- The questions page's branch was merged before the plan, again before the column's task, and before
  the final review, as the approval comment asked, so the column, its session, and quoting are that
  branch's.
- The column keeps the questions page's width, 480 px at 1440 px, though the approval comment found
  it a bit too big: it is one width for both pages, and the human is to review it on the pull
  request.
- On the phone, a story keeps both its widths behind the Phone and Desktop switch, each scaled to
  fit, where the spec had the phone's width only.
- The questions page's plan's first task holds 6/6 steps, not the spec's 5/5, which came from the
  mockup's data.
- "Since your review" shows only while the spec waits for review, never on an approved spec; History
  is not one of its parts, and a changed part holding a link, an image, or code keeps its drawing,
  marked.
- On the phone, no part's head nor band sticks: the parts are panes as on wide screens, the block's
  record line is its pane's title, and decision panes keep only their titled top border too.
- "Ask the agent" on a decision's part quotes its words without their markdown; the header names the
  file and lines.
- The decisions' heading and Contents count the unlisted changes' panes too: "Decisions · 1 new, 2
  changed, 1 removed" on the fixture.
- The phone's open sheet is in the address, `?tab=`, for the spec's, the plan's, and the round's
  columns alike, through one status line and one sheet hook shared with the round; Contents and the
  round's Outline share one list of rows.
- A spec's or a plan's feature without a branch has no Session tab; the round keeps its blocked one.
- The status line's counts give way to the page's controls where they have no room.
- A live window grows past its column only where the room is wider than the 16 px gap.
- Jumps land right under the top bar for every element with a scroll margin, on every page, not only
  the spec's panes.
