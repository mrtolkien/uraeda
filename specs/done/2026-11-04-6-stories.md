# Record: There shouldn't be a limit of 6 user stories in a feature.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The record of a merged change, not instructions to follow. Started on 2026-11-04, finished on
> 2026-11-04, in [its pull request](https://github.com/mrtolkien/uraeda/pull/95). Its full files are
> in git: `git log -- specs/changes/2026-11-04-6-stories/`.

## What and why

The skill's split rule capped a spec at 6 stories, 30 scenarios, or 2 changed decisions. The human
asked for no hard-coded limit there, "we should keep it generic", and for an agent to offer a split
only "if the implementation of the first feature has real implications on the next step, and should
be done separately".

## Behaviour

- **A split needs a dependency**: the discovery proposes one only when building one part changes how
  the next is designed, so the next part is specified once the first is built. The split is still
  the round's question, with the recommended split as option A.
- **Size is never a reason**: no count of stories, scenarios, or decisions makes a spec too large. A
  spec past the 2,000-word aim moves the build's details to the plan and the rarer scenarios under
  "More scenarios", and keeps the rest.
- **Independent pieces stay in one feature**: a proposal of several independent subsystems, such as
  "reminders, sharing, and a calendar view", is no longer split.
- **The scope is checked again** as the investigation shows more, not only before it.
- **The skill's other limits stay**: the block's 7 items, the spec's 2,000 words, a sentence's 25
  words, a decision's 10 rules, and a scenario's 4 steps. They bound how long a file or a sentence
  is to read, not what a feature holds, and most have a lint in `ura check`.

## Design

The rule lives once, as rule 33 of [Writing for the human](/skills/uraeda/references/writing.md),
and the other steps reuse its words and link it: the discovery's
[scope check](/skills/uraeda/references/discovery.md#scope-check), the spec's
[self-review](/skills/uraeda/references/spec.md#self-review), and the plan's
[scope check](/skills/uraeda/references/plan.md#scope-check) and its reasons to stop. No code
enforced the counts. The fixtures that quote "6 stories" are recorded sessions and rounds, left as
they were.
