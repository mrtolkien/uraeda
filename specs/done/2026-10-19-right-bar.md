# Record: Right bar resizable

> [!NOTE]
>
> The record of a merged change, not instructions to follow. Started on 2026-10-19, finished on
> 2026-10-19, committed to the default branch without a pull request. Its full files are in git:
> `git log -- specs/changes/2026-10-19-right-bar/`.

## What and why

The session's right column, "Progress", and the spec page's "Contents" were meant to resize from
their edge, hide easily, and slide in and out, as the shell's redesign asked
([`2026-10-16-redesign-interface`](2026-10-16-redesign-interface.md)). In the app there was no drag
bar, and hiding removed the column at once.

## Behaviour

- A bar runs along the column's left edge, as tall as the window: dragging it resizes the column
  from 280 to 560 px, its width kept in the browser, and its grip shows on hover, as the left
  sidebar's does.
- Folding, from its button or `]`, leaves a 48 px rail, as wide as the folded sidebar, instead of
  hiding the column. The button keeps its spot in both states.
- The width eases over 200 ms as the column folds or unfolds, and at once under reduced motion.
- A click on the rail's empty space unfolds the column; a click on a control or a link in it does
  not.
- The rail of Progress shows the step's icon, named by the step, and the plan's task count while the
  implementation goes on. The rail of Contents shows the total of its comments, when there are any.
- On the phone the column is still not drawn.

## Design

The handle of Astryx's `ResizeHandle` is 100% tall, and `SideColumn` drew it in a `Layout` slot of
automatic height: it was 0 px tall, its grip 19 px above the page. The old test resized from the
keyboard only, so nothing saw it. The handle is now drawn over the column's start edge, in a sticky
wrapper as tall as the window, and a test drags it with the pointer.

The column stays mounted, so its width can ease:
[`SideColumn`](/packages/web/src/layout/SideColumn.tsx) draws its content, or its rail, in a panel
whose width is the resized width or the rail's. Its users pass the rail: `SessionRail` in
[`SessionAside.tsx`](/packages/web/src/SessionAside.tsx) and `ContentsRail` in
[`SpecContents.tsx`](/packages/web/src/spec/SpecContents.tsx). The left sidebar and its code are
unchanged, and the two do not share a component.

## Changes after approval

- The old "hidden" state is gone: tests that expected a hidden column to unmount now expect the
  rail.
