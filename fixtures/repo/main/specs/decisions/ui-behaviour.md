# UI behaviour

> "I act on things where they are."

## Rules

- A row that leads somewhere is tappable as a whole. _So:_ never only through a button or a
  chevron.
- A small edit, like a line's text, happens in place. _So:_ renaming a task opens no dialog.

## Why

The human acts on what they are looking at, without a detour through a dialog.

## Alternatives considered

- A dialog for every edit: the list hides behind it.

## Applies to

- The web UI's pages
